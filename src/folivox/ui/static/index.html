Sex
Pronunciations names and invented words, read your way
  • One entry per line: word = how to read it. Respell the word the way it sounds (Kvothe = Quothe). A * at the end keeps any ending (Tolkien* = Tolkeen). Lines starting with # are comments.
  • Enter each form of a word yourself (Mirandel, Mirandels): the stress can move between forms. Use * only for names whose stem does not change.
  • Stress: + before the stressed vowel (Mir+andel) or ' right after it (Mira'ndel). Russian works the same way and also takes one capital letter. Check it by ear: the model does not always follow.
  • Local engine only: phonemes between slashes, then a respelling for online engines (Kvothe = /K W OW1 TH/ | Quothe). Online engines ignore stress marks and phonemes.
