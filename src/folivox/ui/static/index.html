Engines

The programs that turn your book into audio. By default all of them run free on this computer; a choice marked cloud sends data to an online service and costs money.

Paid online services · optional

Keys for online voice services. You only need them for “Generate with a provider” in the voice section (making a voice sample when you have no recording) or if you pick a cloud engine above. Without keys the app works fully on this computer. Keys are kept in your computer's keychain, never in a file, and are never shown again.
